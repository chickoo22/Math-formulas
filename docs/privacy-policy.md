# MathVerse Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** [DATE]

MathVerse is an offline-first mathematics and physics study app. This policy explains how the app handles data.

## Information stored on your device

MathVerse stores your bookmarks, revision notes, and calculation history locally on your device. This information is used only to provide the app's study features. It is not sent to MathVerse servers because the app does not operate a user account or cloud-sync service.

You can delete this locally stored information by deleting individual notes or bookmarks, clearing app storage, or uninstalling the app.

## Advertising

MathVerse uses Google AdMob to display advertising when your device is connected to the internet. Google and its advertising partners may collect or process device and advertising information in accordance with their own policies to serve, measure, and improve ads.

Learn more about how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

MathVerse does not sell your personal notes or bookmarks.

## Children

MathVerse is designed for students. Before publishing, the developer will configure the Play Console target audience and advertising settings to match the intended age group. If the app is directed to children, advertising will be configured to comply with applicable Google Play and AdMob requirements.
