MathVerse Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

MathVerse is an offline-first mathematics and physics study app. This policy explains how the app handles data.

Information stored on your device

MathVerse stores your bookmarks, revision notes, and calculation history locally on your device to provide study features. MathVerse does not provide user accounts or cloud-sync services, and this study information is not sent to MathVerse servers.

You can remove local information by deleting individual notes or bookmarks, clearing app storage, or uninstalling the app.

Advertising

MathVerse uses Google AdMob to show advertising when a device is connected to the internet. Google and its advertising partners may collect or process device and advertising information to serve, measure, and improve ads, subject to their own policies.

Learn how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

MathVerse does not sell your personal notes or bookmarks.

Children

MathVerse is designed for students. The developer will configure the Play Console target audience and advertising settings for the app's intended age group and applicable Google Play and AdMob requirements.

Changes to this policy

This policy may change when the app's features, data practices, or advertising configuration change. Changes will be posted on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact support@mathverse.app.
